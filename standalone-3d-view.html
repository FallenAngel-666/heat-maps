    Fix "Error constructing CesiumWidget" in Chrome

    ℹ️

    CesiumJS requires WebGL to render photorealistic Google 3D tiles and satellite imagery. When Chrome's hardware graphics acceleration is turned off or blocked, this error appears.

    Quick 30-Second Fix (Chrome):

    1. Open a new tab and paste: chrome://settings/system
    2. Turn ON: "Use graphics acceleration when available".
    3. Click the "Relaunch" button that appears next to it.
    4. Refresh this page — the photorealistic 3D twin will load immediately!
    Tip: If it still doesn't load after relaunching, visit chrome://gpu to check your graphics status, or enable chrome://flags/#ignore-gpu-blocklist.
