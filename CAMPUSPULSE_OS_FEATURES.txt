5. CAMPUS OPERATIONAL HEALTH SYSTEM (RAG STATUS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CampusPulse OS tracks campus condition through four operational health states:
- Green (Normal): Nominal operational status; all electrical, mechanical, and safety
  subsystems within standard operating parameters.
- Amber (Attention Required): Caution flagged; minor telemetry anomalies, vibration,
  or non-critical maintenance tickets active.
- Red (Critical): Urgent attention required; active fire alarm, power surge, fiber
  cut, or emergency event.
- Grey (No Data / Calibration): Sensor unmonitored or undergoing scheduled calibration.

Dynamic Color Synchronization:
When an incident is reported, the affected building immediately updates its 3D color,
rooftop beacon, and status badge in real time. Resolving the incident restores the
building to its nominal health state.
